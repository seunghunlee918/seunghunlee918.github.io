**Student Supervision and Letters**

<span style="font-size:12pt;">**Before Asking**</span><br>
<span style="font-size:11pt;">Whether you are asking me to be your advisor or to write a recommendation letter, I expect that you have taken at least one of my courses, worked with me on research (as a co-author or research assistant), or met with me on a few occasions to discuss your research interests.</span><br>


<span style="font-size:12pt;">**Advising**</span><br>
<span style="font-size:11pt;">I supervise students working on topics related to development economics, public economics, and political economy, and I am open to other topics in other areas of applied economics, broadly defined. I am interested in empirical projects using quasi-experimental methods or field and survey experiments. </span><br>

<span style="font-size:11pt;">If I agree to advise you, I expect you to meet with me regularly to present your progress, either one-on-one or in group meetings when I advise several students, and to keep to the milestones we agree upon. </span><br>


<span style="font-size:12pt;">**Recommendation Letters**</span><br>
<span style="font-size:11pt;">I am happy to write letters to support your case for academic conferences, graduate programs, and job applications. Please ask at least **4 weeks** before your deadline. In order to write a detailed letter, I may ask you to provide additional materials.  </span><br>

<span style="font-size:11pt;">I can write the strongest letters when I can speak in detail about your abilities without reservation. Thus, I give priority to students I have advised over a substantial period. Requests from other students are considered case by case. </span><br>
